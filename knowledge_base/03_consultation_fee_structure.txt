Consultation charges vary across specialties based on clinical tier and doctor seniority across Practo partner clinics. General Medicine consultations range from INR 500 to INR 800, Pediatrics ranges from INR 600 to INR 900, and Dermatology ranges from INR 700 to INR 1200. Surgical and advanced specialties such as Orthopedics range from INR 800 to INR 1500, while Cardiology consultations range from INR 1000 to INR 2000. All fees include basic vitals triage and digital prescription generation.
