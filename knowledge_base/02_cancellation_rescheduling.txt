Appointments can be rescheduled or cancelled without fee penalties up to two hours prior to the scheduled consultation time. Cancellations made within two hours of the appointment window incur a late-cancellation fee equal to twenty percent of the consultation fee. If a patient does not arrive within fifteen minutes after their scheduled slot, the appointment is marked as a No-Show. Rescheduled appointments retain the original payment receipt and may be shifted up to seven days forward.
