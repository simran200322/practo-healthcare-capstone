Cashless insurance claims are supported exclusively at network-empanelled clinics for eligible pre-approved outpatient procedures and consultations. Patients must present their physical or digital health insurance card along with a government photo ID at the clinic helpdesk prior to consultation. For non-network clinics, patients can download itemized invoices, payment receipts, and signed medical diagnosis summaries from the Practo dashboard within 24 hours to file reimbursement claims directly with their insurer.
